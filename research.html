Feynman integrals & special functions

Analytic evaluation of multi-loop and multi-scale Feynman integrals through N-fold Mellin-Barnes representations, their geometry (conic hulls, triangulations), GKZ hypergeometric systems and the method of brackets.

Mellin-Barnes · hypergeometric functions · conformal integrals

Higgs physics & BSM phenomenology

Extended scalar sectors (2HDM, N2HDM, Higgs triplets) confronted with LHC data, including the di-photon excesses near 95 and 152 GeV, differential top-quark distributions and correlations with EDMs.

collider phenomenology · new scalars · LHC anomalies

EFTs & computational tools

Renormalization-group evolution in SMEFT and leptoquark models, two-loop anomalous dimensions, and open-source Mathematica packages for precision calculations.

SMEFT · RG evolution · computer algebra

Software

Open-source Mathematica packages I have developed with my collaborators.

MBConicHulls

Analytic evaluation of N-fold Mellin-Barnes integrals as (multivariable) series, using conic hulls and triangulations of point configurations.

GitHub arXiv:2012.15108

FeynGKZ

Solving Feynman integrals via Gel’fand-Kapranov-Zelevinsky hypergeometric systems, producing series solutions valid in different kinematic regions.

GitHub arXiv:2211.01285

HyperPrecision

High-precision numerical evaluation of multivariate hypergeometric functions, including outside their region of convergence.

GitHub arXiv:2605.30216
